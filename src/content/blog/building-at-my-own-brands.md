It would be easy to write about Springboard as a theory. I'd rather prove it first.

So I'm building to Springboard at my own brands, starting now, and documenting it here as a public build log.

## Why my own brands

Three reasons.

**It keeps me honest.** Advice is cheap when you don't have to live with the results. When it's my catalog, my email list and my time, I find out quickly what actually works.

**It shows the messy middle.** Most AI case studies skip from "before" to "after." The useful part is in between: what broke, what needed rework, what took longer than expected.

**It's a real test of the claim.** If one operator, or a small team, really can run what used to take a roster of vendors, my own businesses are the cleanest place to show it.

## What I'll share

- The starting point: where each brand scored on the five checkpoints.
- What I built for each checkpoint, in what order, and why.
- What it cost in time and tools.
- What didn't work.

What I won't share is anything that belongs to someone else, or numbers I can't stand behind. When I can't show something, I'll say so.
