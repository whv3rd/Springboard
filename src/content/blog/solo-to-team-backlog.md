Will's notes so far:

- Taking this from a solo operator who wants to do it all on Shopify, to a mid-tier business with a small team.
- At that tier the issue usually isn't development. They have developers, internal or outsourced, who are already good at agentic development. They're stuck in other areas.
- That's where a project management, task tracking or software development lifecycle tool like Jira or Linear needs to be integrated into all of this:
  - to keep track of what you're doing, and show it with tools that are more than vibe-coded Claude reports;
  - for audit-level tracking, like time spent, if you have it in something like Jira;
  - so multiple developers can work on multiple things, connected to your repo and source control;
  - so you can take feedback and requests from others.
- At this tier there's usually a business team with strong opinions, and those may conflict with your priorities. So everything needs to go into a backlog.
