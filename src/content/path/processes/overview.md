Processes is checkpoint 5. It's your core workflows mapped, run through the MCPs you've connected, and repeatable by any operator.

This is the checkpoint that turns the other four into a business that runs without heroics. If a workflow only works when one specific person does it, you haven't reached Springboard yet.

## What it is

Your core workflows are the jobs the store can't run without: a product launch, a promo, a restock, the weekly trading report. Mapping one means writing it down so anyone could follow it: what triggers it, what each step produces, which tool and which connection each step uses, and where a person has to make a call.

Once a workflow is mapped and the tools are connected, Claude can run most of it. You make the decisions. It does the work in between.

## Why it comes last

You can't run a workflow through tools that aren't connected yet. A launch touches the design system, the store and the marketing platform; it can only run end to end once all three are connected.

But writing your workflows down doesn't have to wait. That's a 2 on this checkpoint, and you can do it any time. It makes every other track easier, because you'll know which jobs you're connecting the tools for.

## What changes once it's in place

- Your main operator can take two weeks off and the store keeps running, with the same results.
- The weekly trading report gets produced every week from your connected tools. You read it and act on it.
- A new operator learns the business from the maps, not by shadowing one person until they've seen everything.
- When something goes wrong, there's a written process to fix, not a habit to argue about.

## How it connects to the other checkpoints

Every workflow runs through one or more of the other four:

- **Design system (1)** and **AI ↔ design (2)** for anything people see.
- **Platform (3)** for product, catalog and order work.
- **Marketing (4)** for campaigns, segments and flows.

A process can only be as connected as the weakest tool it runs through.

## Who it's for

- **Solo operators.** The person who would be out for two weeks is you. Maps are how the business survives you having a life, or how you hand work to a first hire.
- **Small teams.** Maps are what make "anyone on the team" true. Without them, each workflow belongs to whoever usually does it.
