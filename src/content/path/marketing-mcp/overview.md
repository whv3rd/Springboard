Marketing MCP is checkpoint 4. It's your email and SMS platform connected to Claude, so it can work on your campaigns, flows and segments with you.

The Path uses Klaviyo, because that's where I'd start a store of this size. The thinking applies to similar platforms; the specifics of the connection won't.

## What it is

An MCP connection between Claude and your marketing platform, plus the material Claude needs to do the work well: your calendar, your brand voice, your product data and your design system.

With that in place, the person with the idea can carry it through to a drafted campaign or a working segment, without waiting on a specialist.

## Why it matters

Marketing is where small teams feel the squeeze most. Campaigns need writing, building and scheduling every week. Segments need thinking through. Flows get set up once and then quietly go stale, because nobody has time to look at them.

That's work AI connected to the account can carry a lot of, as long as a human still decides what goes out.

## What changes once it's in place

- Campaigns get drafted from the calendar, not written from scratch the night before.
- Segments get described in plain words, and built and checked against the account.
- Flows like welcome, abandoned cart and post-purchase get reviewed on a schedule, not left as they were at setup.
- Every send still gets a human yes. That doesn't change.

## How it connects to the other checkpoints

- **Design system (1)** and **AI ↔ design (2)** keep campaign emails and creative on-brand.
- **Platform (3)** feeds product data into campaigns, so a clean, connected catalog makes better ones.
- **Processes (5)**: a promo or a product launch runs across the store and the marketing platform together, which is where mapped workflows pay off.

## Who it's for

- **Solo operators.** Regular campaigns and well-kept flows stop depending on whether you had a spare evening.
- **Small teams.** Whoever owns marketing spends their time on the calendar, the offer and the review, not on building each email by hand. Approval stays with one person.
