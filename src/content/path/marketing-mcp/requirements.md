Your marketing platform holds your customer list and can send to all of it. Get these in place before you connect anything.

## Accounts and tools

- A Klaviyo account, or a similar email and SMS platform, already connected to your store.
- Owner or admin access, so you can create the API credentials the connection needs.
- A Claude plan that lets you connect MCP servers.

## Data and material

- Your list in good shape: consent recorded properly, and nothing in it you shouldn't be sending to.
- An inventory of your flows (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase and any others) and what each one is meant to do.
- Your campaign calendar, even a rough one.
- Brand voice notes, for anything Claude writes.
- Email templates built from your design system, so drafts come out on-brand. See the [design system track](/path/design-system/overview).
- Clean product data helps a lot. See the [platform track](/path/platform-mcp/overview).

## People and time

- One person who approves every send. On a solo store, that's you.
- Someone who keeps an eye on deliverability and list health.
- Time to review AI drafts properly at first, while you learn where they need the most help.

## Decisions to make first

- **Draft or schedule.** Whether Claude only drafts campaigns, or can also schedule them once a human has approved. Either way, a human decides what goes out.
- **Scope.** Which of campaigns, segments and flows Claude works on first.
- **Customer data.** What customer information Claude needs to see to do the job, and what it doesn't.
- **Review rhythm.** How often your flows get looked at, and who signs off a change to one.
- **Your first job.** One campaign or segment to build together first.
