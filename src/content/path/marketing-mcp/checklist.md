The [Score](/score) rates this checkpoint from two questions: how campaigns get built in your email and SMS platform, and how your automated flows are looked after. This is what each rating looks like in practice.

## 0 or 1: not started, or ad hoc

- No regular campaigns, or someone writes one when there's time.
- No flows running, or a welcome flow from setup that nobody has touched since.

## A 2: in place, not connected to AI

- [ ] Campaigns go out on a calendar, built by hand or by an agency.
- [ ] A full set of flows is running: welcome, abandoned cart and post-purchase at least.
- [ ] Flows are reviewed and tuned by hand.
- [ ] Every send has a named approver.

## A 3: connected, used sometimes

Everything in a 2, plus:

- [ ] AI connected to the platform drafts some campaigns or segments.
- [ ] AI with access to the account audits or edits your flows sometimes.
- [ ] You've decided what the connection can change, and nothing sends without a human yes.

## A 4: connected, and part of how work gets done

Everything in a 3, plus:

- [ ] AI connected to the platform builds most campaigns and segments. You approve them.
- [ ] AI reviews and updates your flows on a schedule, not just when someone remembers.
- [ ] Campaign emails and creative come out on-system, from your design system.
- [ ] Campaigns draw on live product data, not copied-in details.

## You've earned a 4 when…

- The calendar gets filled without anyone writing every email from scratch.
- A new segment goes from idea to built in one conversation.
- Your flows have been looked at recently, and you know when they'll be looked at next.
- Every send still has a human yes, and that yes is quick, because the draft was right.
