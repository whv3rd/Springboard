The [Score](/score) rates this checkpoint from two questions: whether an AI assistant is connected to your Figma files, and who made the first draft the last time you needed a landing page or a set of ad creatives. This is what each rating looks like in practice.

## 0 or 1: not started, or ad hoc

- Nothing is connected. At most, screenshots get pasted into a chat.
- First drafts come from an agency or freelancer working from a brief, or you make them by hand with an AI image or copy tool on the side.

## A 2: in place, not connected to AI

- [ ] You have the AI account and a tidy Figma file, but they aren't connected.
- [ ] First drafts are made in Figma, from your own components, without AI.
- [ ] Your design system is at a 2 or better, so there's something worth connecting to.

## A 3: connected, used sometimes

Everything in a 2, plus:

- [ ] Claude, or a similar assistant, is connected to your Figma files through an MCP server or plugin.
- [ ] You use it now and then for pages or assets.
- [ ] AI has drafted pages in Figma from your components, though you still fix a fair bit.
- [ ] Your rules are written down and given to the AI when you use it.

## A 4: connected, and part of how work gets done

Everything in a 3, plus:

- [ ] Working with AI in Figma is part of the weekly routine for pages and assets.
- [ ] AI drafts new pages and creative on-system: your components, your variables, your templates.
- [ ] Drafts are reviewed and shipped the same day, not rebuilt.
- [ ] When a draft goes wrong, you fix the rules or the system, so it doesn't happen again.

## You've earned a 4 when…

- Your first move on a new page or a set of ad creatives is a conversation, not a brief.
- AI drafts arrive using your real components, not lookalikes.
- Review is about choices, not repairs.
- A landing page can go from idea to reviewed and shipped in a day.
