Today I lead digital experience and technology at a mid-sized ecommerce brand, consult with founders and leadership teams on replatforms and digital operations, and run a portfolio of my own brands that I'm building to Springsboard now.

Everything on this site comes from that work. The standard is practitioner, not pundit: real workflows from live stores, with real numbers, and a plain note when something can't be shared.
